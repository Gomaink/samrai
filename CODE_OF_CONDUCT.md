# Code of conduct

The samrai project should be a place where people can discuss code, reading software, and self-hosting without being talked down to or harassed.

Be clear and respectful. Critique the change, not the person. Assume honest mistakes, ask for context when something is unclear, and stop an argument before it becomes personal.

The following are not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, or discriminatory language;
- sexualized comments or unwanted attention;
- publishing private information;
- deliberate disruption of issues or reviews;
- repeated hostile behavior after a maintainer asks for it to stop.

Maintainers may edit, hide, lock, or remove contributions that break these rules. Serious or repeated behavior may lead to a temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.

Report conduct problems privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be handled with as much confidentiality as the circumstances allow.
